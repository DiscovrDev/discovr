Profile context is everything that belongs to **this viewer** — their watchlist, what they've rated, where they paused, what they've watched. It's the layer that makes your app feel personal rather than generic.

After selecting a profile, you pull this data to enrich your UI: progress bars on thumbnails, a "Continue Watching" row, a filled heart on the title sheet. This is separate from the recommendation rows on a page — those are chosen by Discovr; profile context is the explicit state your app writes and reads.

The main areas:

- **Continue watching** — the **playback** list: [`getPlaybackItems()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-playback-items) and [`deletePlaybackItem()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/delete-playback-item). Live positions are updated via `client.trackPlayback(item)` (backed by [`scrobble()`](/sdk-reference/scrobble/scrobble)).
- **Ratings, watchlist, and history** — read any list with [`getTitles()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-titles) (or the convenience wrappers `getLiked()`, `getWatchlist()`, and `getHistory()`), and update them with [`setRating()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/set-rating), [`clearRating()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/clear-rating), [`addToWatchlist()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/add-to-watchlist), and [`removeFromWatchlist()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/remove-from-watchlist). **Every state write returns the title's updated `TitleState`**, so you can update your UI straight away without refetching `getTitleState()`.
- **Per-title overlay** — [`getTitleState(item)`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-title-state) for rating, watchlist, resume position, watch history, and completion. See [Understanding your media state](/guides/profile-context/media-profile-meta).
- **Per-season overlay** — [`getSeasonState(item, seasonNumber)`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-season-state) for per-episode completion within a single TV season. See [Finished episodes with getSeasonState](/guides/profile-context/season-profile-meta).
- **Live playback sync and marking watched** — `client.trackPlayback(item)` (or low-level [`scrobble()`](/sdk-reference/scrobble/scrobble)), [`markWatched()`](/sdk-reference/scrobble/mark-watched), and [`markUnwatched()`](/sdk-reference/scrobble/mark-unwatched) keep watch history, completion, and resume position in sync. See [Tracking playback with scrobbling](/guides/profile-context/scrobbling).
- **Watch-history imports** — [`importHistory()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/import-history) backfills titles watched elsewhere with an `imported` source label.

| What you want to show          | API to call                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Progress bar / "Resume" button | [`getTitleState()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-title-state)                                                                                                     |
| "Continue Watching" row        | [`getPlaybackItems()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-playback-items)                                                                                               |
| Filled ♥ / watchlist icon     | [`getTitleState()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-title-state)                                                                                                     |
| Update rating or watchlist     | [`setRating()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/set-rating), [`addToWatchlist()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/add-to-watchlist) (both return `TitleState`)              |
| Keep "Continue Watching" fresh | [`scrobble()`](/sdk-reference/scrobble/scrobble) during playback                                                                                                      |
| "Completed" badge / checkmark  | [`getTitleState()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-title-state) (title/season) or [`getSeasonState()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-season-state) (per-episode) |

## Lists (ratings, watchlist, history)

All profile lists come from **[`getTitles()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-titles)** with one filter (`rating`, `watchlist: true`, or `watched: true`), ordered most recent first. The SDKs also provide thin wrappers — `getLiked()`, `getDisliked()`, `getSuperLiked()`, `getWatchlist()`, and `getHistory()` — that pass the right filter for you.

Items in every list — and in Continue Watching — carry a **`ref`** next to their `id`. When your user opens an item from a list, or acts on it, pass the `ref` (see [passing the `ref` when your user acts](/guides/rows#pass-the-ref-when-your-user-acts)). A bare `id` always works.

## Rating a title

Like, dislike, and super-like are one three-way rating: **not for me** (`disliked`), **I like this** (`liked`), and **love this** (`super_liked`). A title holds at most one rating at a time. Calling [`setRating()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/set-rating) replaces any previous rating automatically, and **returns the title's updated `TitleState`** so you can update your UI immediately without refetching. Calling [`clearRating()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/clear-rating) clears it and also returns `TitleState`.

A super-like isn't a bonus on top of a like. It replaces it, which is why "love this" is the natural label.

Build it as a single control with three states, driven by `state.rating` on [`getTitleState()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/get-title-state). Tapping the active state calls `clearRating()`.

| The viewer says | Call                                                                           | Clear                                                               |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Not for me      | [`setRating(mediaId, "disliked")`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/set-rating)    | [`clearRating(mediaId)`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/clear-rating) |
| I like this     | [`setRating(mediaId, "liked")`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/set-rating)       | [`clearRating(mediaId)`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/clear-rating) |
| Love this       | [`setRating(mediaId, "super_liked")`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/set-rating) | [`clearRating(mediaId)`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/clear-rating) |

The watchlist works the same way: [`addToWatchlist()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/add-to-watchlist) and [`removeFromWatchlist()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/remove-from-watchlist) are independent of ratings and both return the title's updated `TitleState`.

<Info>
	Ratings shape the recommendations a viewer gets. Likes and super-likes steer their rows toward
	titles they'll enjoy, and a dislike steers them away. That makes the control worth building well:
	it's how a viewer teaches Discovr what they enjoy.
</Info>

## History vs playback

**History** (`getTitles({ watched: true })` / `getHistory()`) returns one entry per watched title, newest first, with `completed`, `plays`, and (for TV) the most recent `season` and `episode`. **Playback** (`getPlaybackItems()`) drives “Continue Watching” and holds the viewer's live place in each title, so a title's resume position is always the latest one, on every device.

Use **[`scrobble()`](/sdk-reference/scrobble/scrobble)** during live playback, **[`markWatched()`](/sdk-reference/scrobble/mark-watched)** and **[`markUnwatched()`](/sdk-reference/scrobble/mark-unwatched)** for manual watched/unwatched toggles, and **[`importHistory()`](/sdk-reference/profile-lists/import-history)** when importing watch history from an external source.

**Completion** is computed from what's been watched: a movie or episode counts as complete at 80% progress or more, a season is complete once every aired episode in it is complete, and a show is complete once every regular season (season 1 and up — specials don't count) is complete. See [Understanding your media state](/guides/profile-context/media-profile-meta#completion-rules).
